What WCRS is

A forum for unpublished work on technology entrepreneurship, built to improve it

The symposium aspires to provide an intellectually stimulating experience, map the theoretical domain of technology entrepreneurship, and build community. Around fifty to sixty scholars gather each year; participants come from the sponsoring schools, other West Coast universities, and the rest of the world.

Working papers

The steering committee invites unpublished working papers that advance knowledge on technology entrepreneurship. Papers on entrepreneurship and innovation in technology-based settings are given preference.

Faculty present

WCRS rules require faculty members to present. Submissions with graduate-student co-authors are welcomed, and a faculty co-author is expected to attend and present.

Doctoral consortium

A consortium for doctoral students from the sponsoring schools precedes the main conference, with mentoring from faculty and a first look at the field's current work.

West Coast Research Symposium

An annual gathering of researchers of technology entrepreneurship, initiated in Seattle in 2003 and sponsored by six schools: Stanford University, University of Alberta, UC Santa Barbara, University of Oregon, University of Southern California, and University of Washington. Registration runs through the host school each year.

WCRS is a working-paper conference. Authors share drafts so that colleagues can read them before the sessions and improve them afterwards. Papers are shared with participants directly and are not hosted on this site.
