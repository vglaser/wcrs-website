West Coast Research Symposium

An annual gathering of researchers of technology entrepreneurship, initiated in Seattle in 2003 and sponsored by six schools: Stanford University, University of Alberta, UC Santa Barbara, University of Oregon, University of Southern California, and University of Washington. Registration runs through the host school each year.

WCRS is a working-paper conference. Authors share drafts so that colleagues can read them before the sessions and improve them afterwards. Papers are shared with participants directly and are not hosted on this site.
