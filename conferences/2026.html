Call for papers

WCRS 2026 Submissions closed May 15, 2026

We invite authors to submit papers to our research symposium, a lively forum for the exchange of ideas among scholars interested in technology entrepreneurship. The 2026 conference will be held at The Banff Centre in Banff, Alberta, a conference centre in the Canadian Rockies, hosted by the Alberta School of Business at the University of Alberta.

Papers focusing on entrepreneurship and innovation in technology-based settings will be given preference. In addition to our broad focus on technology entrepreneurship, for 2026 we especially encourage submissions from authors examining entrepreneurial resilience and agentic AI: how algorithmic agents, autonomous technologies, and AI-driven systems are reshaping entrepreneurial processes, organizational routines, and technology ventures. We are open to a wide variety of perspectives on these topics.

We expect to host an intimate conference of 50+ leading scholars from around the world. The conference steering committee invites the submission of unpublished working papers that advance the state of knowledge on technology entrepreneurship. Please note that WCRS rules require faculty members to present. Thus, while submissions that include graduate student co-authors are welcomed and encouraged, a faculty co-author will be expected to attend and present.

We hope to see you in Banff!

Submissions are handled by the University of Washington (Emily Cox Pahnke).

Travel and venue

The Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity

The Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity · 107 Tunnel Mountain Drive, Banff, Alberta, Canada · Tel. 403.907.9849

The Banff Centre offers on-site accommodation at a group rate for conference attendees; registration and accommodation details are shared with accepted authors, and participants book their own lodging. Attendees are free to explore the town of Banff on the evening of September 9.

Getting to the Banff Centre

Calgary International Airport is the closest airport to Banff, a two-hour drive away. The Banff Airporter is the official airport shuttle provider of the Banff Centre and drops travellers at the Professional Development Centre, where the front desk is located; book at banffairporter.com or by phone at 403.762.3330 (1.888.449.2901 toll-free in North America). Brewster Express also offers daily motor-coach shuttles from the airport to Banff; reservations are required.

Park pass and getting around

All visitors need a park pass to enter Banff National Park. Buy one in advance from the Town of Banff or on arrival at the Banff Visitor Centre. The town's transportation page covers local transit.

On foot from the Centre

Campus trails, 20–30 minutes. Surprise Corner viewpoint over the Bow Valley, 30–40 minutes. Bow River and Bow Falls, 45–60 minutes. Into the town of Banff, 35–45 minutes: downhill out, uphill back. September is elk rut; keep well back and give them the trail. Banff sits at roughly 1,400 metres, so dress in layers.

West Coast Research Symposium

An annual gathering of researchers of technology entrepreneurship, initiated in Seattle in 2003 and sponsored by six schools: Stanford University, University of Alberta, UC Santa Barbara, University of Oregon, University of Southern California, and University of Washington. Registration runs through the host school each year.

WCRS is a working-paper conference. Authors share drafts so that colleagues can read them before the sessions and improve them afterwards. Papers are shared with participants directly and are not hosted on this site.
